# Email Awesome top-up interface check — 2026-10-20

## Source and scope

- Official authenticated product: [My Subscription](https://app.emailawesome.com/billing).
- Reviewed the visible billing controls and an explicitly authorized synthetic Bulk preview. No customer data, transaction details or authenticated screenshots are included in this public repository.

## Observed in the product

- A purchased balance is displayed separately from the monthly allowance.
- **Buy more verifications** offers one-time credits with **Buy credits →** and quantity controls.
- The offer showed 2,000 verifications for $14, $7 per 1,000 and a 2,000 minimum. One increase selected 5,000 for $35; the control was returned to its original quantity. These observations are dated, not fixed pricing or a verified currency code.
- The card states **No expiry · API, bulk and single checks**. This records the product's displayed terms; no long-term expiry or debit-priority behavior was tested.
- Bulk Verifications accepted a CSV with 1,001 unique synthetic `example.com` addresses. **Verify** opened a preview with 1,001 addresses and zero syntax/duplicate exclusions; final validation remained a separate **Confirm** action.
- With insufficient capacity, the preview showed **Not enough credits to run this list**, required/available/missing counts, **Upgrade plan**, a **Buy 2,000 credits · $14** option and disabled **Confirm**.
- **Cancel** preserved the waiting file. **Resume** reopened the same preview without starting validation. After another cancellation, **Discard** cleared only this disposable synthetic pending upload.
- The displayed credit allowance and the existing five Recent Activity entries were unchanged after the test; no new validation job appeared.

## Instruction changes

The product skill now checks existing capacity before proposing a purchase, distinguishes occasional top-ups from recurring plans, uses offered tiers rather than assuming equal increments, requires transaction approval, and re-checks balance and job state before resuming a batch.

## Verification boundary

This was an authenticated interface and preview test. No credits were bought or consumed, no new list was validated, no subscription was changed and no checkout settlement was tested. Reopening the preview with Resume was tested; a paid top-up and execution of a Bulk job after payment were not. Local package checks validate instructions and existing helpers separately from live product execution.
