# Email Awesome skills

Primary product skill:

- `emailawesome` - operate current single and bulk verification through browser/computer use when available, guide Warm-up, reconcile final results and explain visible usage. No MCP is assumed.

Existing technical recipes:

- `emailawesome-list-cleaner` - clean and verify an email list before a campaign
- `emailawesome-hubspot-verification` - verify new HubSpot leads through Zapier before outreach
- `emailawesome-api-validation` - add asynchronous email validation to forms and lead pipelines

Install `emailawesome` first for product use. The technical recipes remain available for their focused tasks while the use-case portfolio is revised.
