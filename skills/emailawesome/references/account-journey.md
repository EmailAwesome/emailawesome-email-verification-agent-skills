# Product account and capacity

1. **Install and connect.** Install the `emailawesome` product skill. Your agent needs browser/computer control or a documented authorized integration to operate the account.
2. **Log in or sign up.** Open [Email Awesome](https://app.emailawesome.com/) or [create an account](https://app.emailawesome.com/signup?plan=free_trial). Reuse an existing account. Complete authentication yourself; do not share passwords in chat.
3. **Use existing credits first.** Inspect the monthly allowance, purchased balance and the product's displayed available total or batch estimate. Purchased credits are shown separately from the monthly allowance; do not assume their debit order or add figures whose scope is unclear. Prepare a small authorized list, exclude suppressed records before upload and use the preview's count rather than raw CSV rows. Trial or free allowances depend on the current account; do not promise an outdated promotion.
4. **Resolve a capacity gap without forcing an upgrade.** For occasional extra volume, inspect the one-time top-up in [My Subscription](https://app.emailawesome.com/billing), under **Buy more verifications**. Compare it with a monthly plan from [current pricing](https://www.emailawesome.com/pricing) when the need is recurring. Use the context already supplied; ask about expected frequency only if it changes the recommendation. If a Bulk warning offers a top-up, inspect that route and its terms instead of assuming the batch requires a new subscription. Follow the purchase and resume checks below.
5. **Verify and reconcile.** Run the agreed batch, wait for final results and join them to source IDs. Preserve VALID, INVALID, CATCH_ALL, UNKNOWN, failed, excluded and pending independently. Verification is the product step; campaign drafts and business decisions are the skills output, and sending is a separate action.

## One-time top-up: inspect, explain, then resume

- Show the batch estimate, usable capacity and the resulting shortfall. Choose the smallest currently offered quantity covering that shortfall, subject to the displayed minimum and the user's budget. The quantity controls may select tiers, not equal increments.
- Read the selected quantity, total price, currency, one-time versus recurring charge, expiry terms and supported verification modes from the live UI. Compare subscription costs on a stated billing period; do not compare a promotional first month with an undiscounted top-up as if both were permanent rates. Do not assume a subscription coupon applies to top-ups.
- Explain the choice before the transaction. An instruction to verify a list, open billing or inspect top-ups does not authorize payment. Obtain approval for the actual amount and respect the agent environment's payment/handoff rules. If no purchase is approved, keep the job pending and offer a user-approved smaller batch or waiting for the next allowance only when that option is suitable and available.
- After the user completes an approved payment, re-read the balance and inspect any credit-history confirmation. A checkout redirect, success URL or an open purchase screen alone does not prove credits were added. If settlement is pending or unclear, do not submit a second payment or claim new capacity.
- Return to the existing Bulk flow, re-check its estimate and completion state, then resume only the already authorized work. If the current screen shows a waiting file with **Resume**, use it to reopen the preview instead of uploading the file again. The observed insufficient-credit preview kept **Confirm** disabled; reopening it did not start validation. Do not assume buying credits automatically starts the job, and do not submit an already completed list again. **Discard** clears the waiting file; do not use it unless the user wants to abandon that pending upload.

These are dated observations, not fixed prices, proof of checkout currency, debit-order guarantees or a completed payment test. Always read the current offer.
